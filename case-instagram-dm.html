Instagram DM Lead Automation

A keyword-triggered ManyChat flow that instantly replies to comments and DMs with a registration link - capturing leads and driving signups to a live workshop on autopilot.

The Challenge

Promoting a live workshop on Instagram created a flood of comments and DMs. Replying to each one with the signup link by hand was impossible to keep up with - and slow replies lost signups.

The client needed every interested person to get the registration link instantly, the moment they engaged, without anyone manning the inbox.

How It Works

  • Triggers: The flow fires when a user DMs a keyword (e.g. LOVE) or comments on a specific post or reel.
  • Instant DM: ManyChat immediately sends a warm, on-brand message in Instagram.
  • Deliver the offer: The message includes the workshop details and a one-tap registration link.
  • Capture: The contact is saved so they can be followed up and reminded before the event.
  • Scale: It handles unlimited interactions at once, 24/7, with no manual replies.
