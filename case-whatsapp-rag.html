WhatsApp AI Assistant (RAG)

A multimodal WhatsApp assistant that answers from a private knowledge base - handling text, voice notes, images, and PDFs, with documents embedded into a MongoDB vector store for accurate retrieval.

The Challenge

Customers ask questions on WhatsApp in every format - typed messages, voice notes, photos, and PDFs - and expect answers grounded in the business's own information, not generic AI guesses.

The client needed one assistant that could understand any message type and reply accurately from their private knowledge base.

How It Works

  • Route by type: A WhatsApp trigger detects whether the message is text, a voice note, an image, or a document.
  • Understand any format: Voice notes are transcribed, images are read with vision, and PDFs are extracted to text.
  • Knowledge base: Business documents are embedded and stored in a MongoDB vector store for retrieval.
  • Retrieve & answer: A knowledge-base agent pulls the most relevant information and composes a grounded reply.
  • Respond: The answer is sent straight back to the customer on WhatsApp.
