Make.comPrestaShopXero

PrestaShop to Xero Order Sync

An order-to-accounting sync that pushes every PrestaShop website order into Xero as contacts and invoices automatically, every 15 minutes - eliminating manual bookkeeping.

Client
Multi-channel retailer
Timeline
10 days
My Role
Integration build

The Challenge

Every website order had to be re-entered into Xero by hand - creating the customer, then the invoice. It was slow, easy to get wrong, and didn't scale with order volume.

The client needed website orders to flow into their accounting system automatically and accurately, with no manual data entry.

How It Works

  • Fetch orders: Every 15 minutes, Make.com pulls new orders from PrestaShop.
  • Aggregate & iterate: Orders are aggregated and processed one by one.
  • Match contact: The customer is found or created as a contact in Xero.
  • Create invoice: A matching invoice is generated in Xero with the order details.
  • Route edge cases: A router handles different order scenarios so nothing slips through.
