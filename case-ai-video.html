AI Video Generation & Auto-Posting

An end-to-end short-video factory: an RSS trigger sparks an AI video idea, generates clips with Seedance, stitches the final cut with Fal AI, and uploads straight to TikTok and YouTube.

The Challenge

Producing short-form video daily - ideation, clips, sound, editing, captions, and uploading to each platform - is a full-time job on its own.

The client wanted a system that took an idea all the way to a published video on TikTok and YouTube with no manual editing or posting.

How It Works

  • Idea: An RSS feed trigger kicks off creative video idea generation, with metadata saved to Google Sheets.
  • Prompt build: Detailed, validated video prompts are generated for each scene.
  • Generate clips: Seedance renders the individual video clips from the prompts.
  • Add sound: ASMR / audio is generated and layered onto the clips.
  • Stitch: Fal AI merges the clips into one final rendered video.
  • Publish: The finished video is delivered and uploaded to TikTok and YouTube.
