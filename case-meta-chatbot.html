Meta Ads AI Chatbot Assistant

A conversational AI assistant built in n8n that answers questions about Meta ad accounts and campaign insights on demand - replying in chat like an on-call media buyer.

Client

Timeline

My Role

The Challenge

Checking ad performance meant logging into Meta Ads Manager, digging through campaigns, and manually pulling numbers - a slow chore repeated many times a day.

The client wanted a chat assistant they could simply ask about their accounts, spend, and ad insights, and get an instant, accurate answer back in plain language.

How It Works

  • Chat trigger: A message in the chat interface starts the n8n agent.
  • Understand: An AI brain interprets the request and decides what data is needed.
  • Account & insight logic: Dedicated tools fetch the right account data and ad insights.
  • Calculate: A calculator step handles any maths - spend, ratios, performance deltas.
  • Reply: The agent responds conversationally with the exact answer, on demand.
