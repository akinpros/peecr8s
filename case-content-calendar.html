The Challenge

Planning content that actually lands means researching competitors and trends across half a dozen platforms - then turning all of it into a coherent calendar. Done by hand it takes days and is mostly guesswork.

The client wanted a single request to trigger deep research and return a ready-to-use, data-backed content plan they could act on immediately.

How It Works

  • Trigger & normalize: A webhook kicks off the engine and the brand's data is normalized into a consistent brief.
  • Multi-platform research: In parallel it researches competitors, content trends, YouTube, Reddit, TikTok, Instagram hashtags, and LinkedIn posts.
  • Aggregate: Every research stream is combined into one rich context set.
  • Generate plan: Anthropic Claude turns that research into a complete, structured content plan.
  • Document & deliver: The plan is written into a Google Doc and the finished content calendar is emailed out.
