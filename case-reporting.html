The Challenge

Reporting meant manually pulling daily and weekly numbers, crunching them, formatting a report, and sending it - a repetitive task that swallowed hours.

The client wanted the whole process to run itself: gather, calculate, format, and deliver, accurately and on schedule.

How It Works

  • Trigger: The workflow runs on a set schedule (or on demand).
  • Gather: n8n pulls both daily and weekly rows of data in parallel.
  • Calculate: Dedicated code steps compute the daily and weekly metrics.
  • Merge & format: Both streams are merged and shaped into one report in JavaScript.
  • Deliver: The finished report is sent out automatically as a message.
