Facebook Messenger AI Bot

A Facebook Messenger assistant that reads and replies to DMs in real time with Gemini - complete with typing indicators, conversation memory, message chunking, and image and video responses.

Client
Facebook page business
Timeline
2 weeks
My Role
Bot architecture & build

The Challenge

A busy Facebook page was getting more DMs than the team could answer. People waited hours, conversations lost context, and long replies got cut off or felt robotic.

The client wanted an assistant that replied instantly and naturally, remembered the conversation, and could send more than just plain text.

How It Works

  • Receive: A Facebook webhook captures each incoming message and verifies it.
  • Human touches: The bot marks the message seen and shows a typing indicator before replying.
  • Understand with memory: Gemini Flash processes the merged message using conversation memory for context.
  • Chunk long replies: Responses over 2000 characters are automatically split so nothing is cut off.
  • Rich responses: A switch decides whether to send text, an image, or a video back to the user.
