The Challenge

Faceless channels live or die on consistent output, but producing each video - theme, script, voiceover, visuals, editing - is hours of repetitive work that is hard to keep up daily.

The client wanted to fire off a single Telegram message and have a finished, on-theme video produced automatically, with clear tracking of what was done.

How It Works

  • Trigger: A Telegram message starts the workflow and loads the run configuration.
  • Pick a theme: The engine extracts and selects the strongest theme to build around.
  • Structured prompt: A detailed, validated video prompt is generated for the piece.
  • Voice & visuals: AI TTS generates the voiceover and the supporting visuals are produced.
  • Assemble & track: The clip is rendered and each job's status is updated to DONE so nothing is lost or duplicated.
