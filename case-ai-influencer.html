The Challenge

Running an AI influencer means producing fresh, on-brand video every day across scripting, visuals, voice, captions, and posting - hours of work daily if done by hand.

The client wanted the whole persona to run itself: pick trending topics, script them, generate the video, and publish everywhere automatically.

How It Works

  • Trigger & filter: An RSS feed of trending topics is scored by a GPT viral filter to pick the strongest ideas.
  • Script: A script + timestamp generator writes the video and its caption structure.
  • Visuals: OpenAI generates the supporting images for the piece.
  • Avatar video: HeyGen renders a lifelike avatar video from the script.
  • Assembly: CloudConvert, Rendi FFmpeg, and JSON2Video stitch and subtitle the final cut.
  • Publish: The finished video is auto-posted to Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok.
