Make.comXeroPrestaShop

Xero to PrestaShop Stock Sync

The inbound half of inventory automation: when a purchase order lands in Xero, it iterates the items and automatically increases the matching product stock on the PrestaShop store, every 15 minutes.

Client
Multi-channel retailer
Timeline
1 week
My Role
Integration build

The Challenge

When new stock was purchased and recorded in Xero, someone still had to manually bump the product quantities on the website - a boring, error-prone job that led to inaccurate stock levels.

The client wanted purchase orders in Xero to automatically top up the matching product stock on their PrestaShop store, with no manual updates.

How It Works

  • Watch: Every 15 minutes, Make.com watches Xero for new purchase orders.
  • Iterate items: Each line item on the purchase order is processed one by one.
  • Look up: The item is fetched from Xero and matched to its product in PrestaShop.
  • Update stock: The matching product's stock is automatically increased on the website.
  • Stay accurate: Website stock now reflects real purchased inventory, continuously.
