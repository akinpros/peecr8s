AI Book Editing Pipeline

A multi-phase manuscript editor that ingests a full book, runs a structural macro-analysis with GPT-5 Mini, and returns a polished editorial blueprint in minutes instead of days.

Client
Author / editor
Timeline

My Role
Pipeline architecture

The Challenge

Producing a macro-level edit of a full manuscript - core idea, structure, themes, pacing - means reading tens of thousands of words and takes a skilled editor days.

The client wanted a repeatable system that takes any .docx manuscript and instantly produces a professional editorial blueprint as the first phase of editing.

How It Works

  • Trigger: A manuscript dropped into Google Drive starts the workflow.
  • Extract: The .docx is downloaded, converted, and its text extracted cleanly.
  • Macro analysis: GPT-5 Mini analyzes core idea, structure, themes, pacing, and weak spots.
  • Format: The output is prepared as a structured Markdown blueprint and converted to a document.
  • Deliver: The finished blueprint is saved back to Google Drive, ready for the next phase.
